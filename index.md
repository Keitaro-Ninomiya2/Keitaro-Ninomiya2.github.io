### Research

My job market paper investigates institutional grading biases and their consequences for access to higher education.

Beyond that, I study labor economics, economics of education, and industrial organization. Methodologically, I combine empirical industrial organization, causal inference, and Bayesian econometrics to understand how micro-level behaviors aggregate into broader economic outcomes.
