### Job Market Paper

My job market paper investigates institutional grading biases and their consequences for access to higher education. I will be presenting at the [ASSA meetings](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2026/preliminary/2143).

### Research Interests

I study labor economics, economics of education, and industrial organization. Methodologically, I combine tools from empirical industrial organization, causal inference, and Bayesian econometrics to understand how micro-level behaviors aggregate into broader economic outcomes.
